**Not multisig!**   

**Not wallet.dat!**    

From years of experimenting with setups, **BIP39 seed words + a passphrase is the answer that fits most people today.** You don't need much time or any arcane jargon to build a setup that is simple and, at the same time, no less secure than multisig (yes — no less!).

> **Creating a wallet = creating seed words + a passphrase**

Building tools and plain-language guides that further lower technical complexity around **BIP39 seed words + passphrase** is the right way to bring Bitcoin to a wider public.
