Day to day, use a **watch-only wallet** to receive coins and keep an eye on the balance. Only when you need to send coins do you enter your seed words + passphrase into a **signing device**.

A signing device can be a hardware wallet or an old phone. Which path fits you?

- **Already own a hardware wallet?** Keep using it — but first read about the [hardware wallet rules of use](/use-wallet/hww) and hold the line on its few ground rules.
- **Don't own a hardware wallet?** Don't bother buying one — grab a spare old Android phone and turn it into an ["hardware" wallet](/use-wallet/old-phone).

> Whichever path you take: generate your seed words yourself with dice ([Create a Wallet](/create-wallet)), and store them per the [four iron rules in Back It Up](/backup-wallet).
